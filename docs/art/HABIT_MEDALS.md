All medals use the same 200 by 200 SVG coordinate system. The face is drawn without a glyph. A separate SVG occupies x=64, y=64, width=72, height=72 and uses a 48 by 48 glyph viewBox. The shared icon catalogue supplies the glyph path and material gradients supply its surface treatment. The renderer accepts `iconId` alongside material, stage and Resonance. Completion belongs to the card corner. Hover, keyboard focus and press styling belong to the card. The registration overlay shows the center and slot on all 18 samples. The compact switch displays every stage at the 94 px card scale.
